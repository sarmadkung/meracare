What it does

Three things, done properly.

Care falls apart in the gaps — a dose nobody logged, an appointment nobody was told about. GenXcare closes those gaps and stays out of the way.

Medications

Schedules, doses, and reminders in one list. Anyone in the circle can mark a dose taken, and everyone sees it — including the missed ones.

Appointments

Every visit with the time, the place, and who is going along. Changes reach the whole circle instead of one person's phone.

The care circle

Invite a sister, a son, a professional caregiver. Each person gets only the access their role needs, and it can be changed or removed at any time.

Who it's for

One app, however your family is arranged.

There is no separate app for professionals and no separate app for families. Roles decide what each person sees.

  • On your own

    Keep your own medications and appointments straight. Nobody else needs an account.

  • With family

    Brothers, sisters, and grandchildren share one plan instead of a group chat.

  • With a caregiver

    A professional caregiver works from the same plan, for one person or for several.

  • Everyone together

    Family and professionals in the same circle, each with their own permissions.

How we build it

Built for the person being cared for.

Large type, plain words

Big touch targets, high contrast, and no jargon anywhere in the app.

Works without signal

Log a dose in a waiting room or a lift. It syncs when the phone reconnects.

Least access by default

People see what their role needs, nothing more. Access can be revoked anytime.

Coordination, not surveillance

GenXcare records the care a family agrees to share. It does not track a person's movements.

Start with one person. Add the others when you're ready.

Set up a plan in a few minutes, then invite whoever helps. GenXcare is free, and there are no ads.
